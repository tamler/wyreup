Add `photo-edit-pro`, a 1-credit hosted Pro tool that edits a photo from a text instruction on FLUX.2 [klein] 4B, with quick actions to remove something, colorize, or restore an old photo. The photo is scaled to fit 512 x 512 before upload and the result is up to 1024 px on the long side. MCP marks it non-idempotent.
